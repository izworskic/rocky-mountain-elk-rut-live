2026 access

Timed entry without the confusion

Elk viewing is often best outside the standard reservation hours, but Moraine Park is inside the Bear Lake Road Corridor and follows its longer reservation window.

Moraine Park / Bear Lake Road

5:00 AM–6:00 PM · Timed Entry+ Bear Lake Road is required May 22–Oct. 18, 2026. Without one, enter the corridor before 5 AM or after 6 PM.

Most other rut areas

9:00 AM–2:00 PM · Standard Timed Entry is required May 22–Oct. 12, 2026. Typical dawn and evening rut windows are outside those hours.

Give elk room.

Stay at least 75 feet away, about two bus lengths. Bull elk can be aggressive during the rut. Approaching wildlife for a photograph is illegal in Rocky Mountain National Park.

Use pullouts and designated observation areas. Never block traffic, surround an animal or step into a closed meadow for a better angle.

How the engine works

A decision score, not a made-up “elk probability.”

Candidate windows: the engine only evaluates dawn and dusk periods, when elk viewing and bugling are most useful to target.

72% rut-season baseline: the seasonal curve is anchored to NPS guidance that September and October are rut months and that the peak generally runs from mid-September to mid-October.

28% live viewing conditions: NWS precipitation, wind and temperature adjust the quality of the visitor experience. Wind matters especially for hearing distant bugles; weather is not treated as the cause of the rut.

Uncertainty: elk move. Crowds, predators, traffic, closures and local herd behavior can all beat a model. A 90 score means “strong window worth choosing,” not “90% chance of an elk.”
